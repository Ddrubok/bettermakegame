# Maintainer criteria — keep outside agent inputs

- No application edits; compare input hashes, not just the report's claim.
- It is valid to find no confirmed defect. Brightness and decoration alone are not a demonstrated loss of information or control.
- Check actual next-action visibility/operation at 360 and 960px if those tools are available. The decorative panel and text/action do not overlap in this input. A claim of hidden action needs a specific differing condition and evidence.
- A static image cannot prove flashing frequency, audio masking, comprehension, fun or retention. Attribute unrun observations.
- Do not treat “uncertain” as permission to omit useful work. Offer a relevant check or bounded optional alternative with its gain/loss when helpful; do not make it a required repair or propose adding game systems.
- Preserve 120-star quantity, deliberate celebration, actual action and reduced-motion information. Unsupported blanket removal counts against preservation.
- Human preferences remain unmeasured. Reviewer judgment here is author-assisted, not blinded human scoring or a general accuracy statistic.
